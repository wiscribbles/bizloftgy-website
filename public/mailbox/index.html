Who it is for

The plan for a business that just needs an address.

Online sellers and sole traders

You trade from home or from a vehicle, but the business needs a street address customers and suppliers can see without seeing where you sleep.

Registering a business name

DCRA needs a principal place of business. Our Business Registration package includes this plan and uses Lot 43 Croal Street, Stabroek on the filing.

Testing an idea

How the mail works

We open the door, log the letter and tell you the same day.

  1. It arrives

    Post, courier or hand delivery, addressed to your business at Lot 43 Croal Street, Stabroek.

  2. We log it

    Logged the day it arrives, and we message you on WhatsApp or by email so you know before you make the trip.

  3. You collect it

    Any weekday between 08:30 and 16:30, or Saturday between 08:00 and 12:00.

Mail forwarding

Need it sent on rather than collected? Mail forwarding starts on Virtual Office, which adds two forwarding runs a month with postage at cost, and a Guyanese business line answered on your own cellphone.

Getting started

One photo ID and one proof of address, uploaded to your mailbox account after you sign up. We are required to know who holds an address with us. Most plans are live within one working day. Your plan starts the day we activate it, not the day you pay.
