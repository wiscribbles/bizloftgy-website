What a virtual office here is — and what it is not

It is

  • A commercial address in central Georgetown you may use on public documents
  • Mail taken in, logged and notified the day it arrives
  • A Guyanese business number with your own extension, ringing on your cellphone
  • A registered office for an incorporated company, with a unit number

It is not

  • A desk or an office you can work from daily — the meeting room is booked by the hour, separately
  • A live answering service: nobody picks up in your business name, the call comes to you
  • A registered-agent service — Virtual Office Plus is offered without it
  • Instant: we need one photo ID and one proof of address before we switch it on
Getting started

Live within a working day, in most cases.

Signup runs on our secure mailbox portal, which is also where you will see your mail and invoices once you are set up.

  1. Sign up

    Choose monthly or annual billing and create your account on the mailbox portal.

  2. Upload two documents

    One photo ID and one proof of address. We are required to know who holds an address with us.

  3. We switch it on

    We activate the plan and send your address-use letter. Your plan starts the day we activate it, not the day you pay.
