Meeting room hire · Georgetown, Guyana

A meeting room in central Georgetown, by the hour.

Ten seats, air conditioning, Wi-Fi, a projector and a whiteboard — and your guests greeted at reception by name. Book it for an hour, a morning, or the whole day.

We set the room up fifteen minutes before you arrive and reset it after you leave. Water and glasses on the table, tea and coffee on the house.

It is a short walk from the High Court, the Deeds Registry and the commercial banks, so the meeting can happen on the same morning as everything else you came into town for.
