Why we exist

Nobody should need a building to look like a business.

Renting an office in Georgetown means a lease, a deposit, a fit-out, a light bill and somebody at the front desk — all of it before the office has earned you a single dollar.

Most businesses don’t need an office. They need an address that isn’t their house, a proper room for the two meetings a month that actually matter, and a business phone they can answer wherever the job takes them.

That is the whole of what we do. You take the part you need, by the hour or by the month, and leave the rest with us.

Questions

Virtual office and meeting room questions.

What is a virtual office?

A virtual office gives your business a real address, mail handling and a business phone line without renting office space. With The Business Loft you use Lot 43 Croal Street, Stabroek, Georgetown on your cards, invoices and website, we receive and log your mail, and you can book our meeting room by the hour when you need to meet clients in person.

How much does a virtual office cost in Georgetown?

Our plans start at GYD 6,000 (US$30) a month for Mailbox Only, GYD 15,000 (US$75) for Virtual Office and GYD 30,000 (US$150) for Virtual Office Plus, at opening rates until 31 December 2026. Each plan has a one-off set-up fee of GYD 1,000, 1,500 or 2,200 respectively. Paying annually covers twelve months for the price of ten. See the full rate card.

Can I register my business at this address?

Yes. Our Business Registration package registers your business name with DCRA, with Lot 43 Croal Street, Stabroek as your principal place of business, and applies for your GRA tax compliance and NIS compliance certificates, for a one-off GYD 30,000 (US$150) plus a Mailbox Only plan. Company incorporation is offered through our partnership with an external attorney-at-law, for GYD 275,000 (US$1,375) paid in cash at our office, with a registered office at Lot 43 Croal Street, Stabroek on our Virtual Office plan. Apply online or see the difference between a business and a company.

Is my mail private?

We log it, secure it and tell you it’s arrived. We don’t open it unless you’ve asked us to scan it, and we release it only to you or to someone you’ve named in writing, against identification.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. The meeting room has no contract at all — book it and that’s that. Plans run month to month, or annually if you’d rather pay for ten months and get twelve, and you can change or cancel with 30 days’ notice.

What do I need to start a plan?

One photo identification — passport, national ID card or driver’s licence — and one proof of address from the last three months, uploaded to your mailbox account after you sign up. Most plans are live within one working day.

Can I see the room before I book?

Come in any weekday between 08:30 and 16:30, or Saturday between 08:00 and 12:00. No appointment, no tour script, no pressure — ask at reception for The Business Loft.

What’s actually included in the meeting room?

Ten seats, air conditioning, Wi-Fi, a wall screen, a whiteboard, water and glasses, and someone at the front to greet your guests. Tea and coffee are on us; printing is extra and priced on the rate card.

Can I book on a Saturday?

Yes, between 08:00 and 12:00, at our standard rates. Book online, or message us. We don’t take evening bookings.

How do I pay?

Plans are paid by card through your online mailbox account, monthly or annually. The meeting room is booked and paid online on our booking page, or in person at reception.
