Policy

Terms of use and booking policy

Last updated 29 September 2026

These terms cover the meeting room and the virtual office plans (Mailbox Only, Virtual Office and Virtual Office Plus) at The Business Loft, 43 Croal Street, Stabroek, Georgetown. Prices are on the rate card, in Guyana dollars.

Plans

  • Starting a plan. A plan needs one photo ID and one proof of address before we switch it on. Your plan starts the day we activate it, not the day you pay; most are live within one working day.
  • Billing. Plans are billed monthly or annually in advance through your mailbox account, which is provided by our mailbox-management partner, SphereMail. Annual plans cost ten months and cover twelve, and the one-off set-up fee is waived.
  • Changing or cancelling. Thirty days’ written notice, at any time, no penalty. Move up a plan whenever you like and we pro-rate the month.
  • Price changes. The rate card is reviewed each 1 January. Any change is capped and notified at least sixty days in advance. The opening rates end on 31 December 2026; from 1 January 2027 the standard rates apply to every client. A plan paid annually before 31 December holds its rate for the twelve months it covers.
  • Using the address. You may use 43 Croal Street as your business address on cards, invoices, letterhead and your website. We do not currently provide registered-office or registered-agent services, and the address may not be filed as a registered office. You may not describe it as premises you occupy full-time, and you may not use it for any unlawful purpose. We may suspend a plan, with notice where the law allows, if the address is misused or identification is not kept current.
  • Mail. We handle your mail as set out in our privacy and mail-handling policy. We do not accept cash, hazardous or perishable items, or anything that requires refrigeration.

Meeting room

  • Booking. Standard hours are Monday to Friday, 08:30–16:30, with a one-hour minimum then half-hours. Off-peak is Saturday, 08:00–12:00, with a two-hour minimum. We don’t take evening bookings. Book online on our booking page (e-commerce.officernd.com/bizloftgy) or at reception.
  • Paying. Pay online when you book, or at reception. First-time bookings may take a 50% deposit with the balance on the day. Plan clients can be invoiced monthly.
  • Changing or cancelling. Free up to 24 hours before. Inside 24 hours we keep half the booking fee; a no-show is charged in full. Message us before you cancel — we would rather move it than charge you.
  • In the room. Ten seats for meetings; up to twelve for Saturday events in an open layout. Please leave the room as you found it. You are responsible for damage caused by you or your guests.
  • Discounts. Virtual Office and Virtual Office Plus clients take 10% off every room rate. Virtual Office Plus includes two room hours a month, which don’t roll over.

This website

You may use this site to find out about our services, book the room and sign up for a plan. Don’t try to interfere with it, probe it for weaknesses without our written permission, or submit anyone else’s details. Security researchers: see security.txt.

Liability

We take reasonable care with your mail, your calls and your bookings. We are not responsible for delays caused by postal services, couriers, telephone networks or events outside our control, and our liability for any claim is limited to the fees you paid us in the three months before it arose, except where the law does not allow that limit.
