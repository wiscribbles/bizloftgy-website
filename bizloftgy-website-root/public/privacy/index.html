Privacy and mail-handling policy

Last updated 29 September 2026

The Business Loft holds other people’s correspondence and answers their calls. This page says what we collect, who we share it with, how we handle your mail, and how to reach us about any of it.

Who we are

The Business Loft, 43 Croal Street, Stabroek, Georgetown, Guyana. Email support@bizloftgy.com, phone (+592) 225-1164, mobile or WhatsApp (+592) 603-2923.

What we collect, and why

  • When you start a plan: your name, business name, email, mobile number, postal address and a password you choose. We need these to open your mailbox account, tell you when mail arrives and bill you.
  • Identification: one photo ID and one proof of address, and a certificate of incorporation if you register a company at our address. We are required to know who holds an address with us, and we keep these for as long as the law requires after your plan ends.
  • When you book the meeting room: your name, contact details, booking times and any set-up notes, so the room is ready and reception can greet your guests.
  • Your mail and calls: a log of each item received (sender, date, type), scans you ask for, and messages taken on your line.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider keeps short-lived server logs (such as IP address and time of request) to keep the site secure. We don’t use advertising trackers on this site.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers, each only for the job described:

  • SphereMail — runs the online mailbox account where you see your mail, upload documents and pay for your plan. Your signup details are sent to SphereMail when you create an account.
  • OfficeRnD — runs the meeting-room booking calendar and checkout.
  • Card payment processors used by SphereMail and OfficeRnD. Card numbers are entered on their secure pages; they never pass through this website and we never see them in full.
  • Netlify — hosts this website. Cloudflare Turnstile — checks that a signup is made by a person, not a bot.

We don’t sell your information and we don’t use your mobile number for marketing. We disclose information to authorities only where the law requires it.

How we handle your mail

  • Every item is logged and kept secure the day it arrives, and you are notified the same day.
  • We do not open your mail unless you have asked us to scan it.
  • Mail is released only to you, or to someone you have named in writing, against photo identification.
  • On Virtual Office Plus, letters from the Deeds Registry, GRA or NIS are flagged and escalated to you within four working hours.
  • When a plan ends, we hold any remaining mail for 30 days for collection or forwarding at cost, then return it to sender.

How long we keep it

Account and billing records: for the life of your plan and as long afterwards as tax and anti-money-laundering rules require. Mail logs and scans: for the life of your plan plus twelve months. Meeting-room bookings: two years. Server logs: a few weeks.

Your choices

You can ask for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete what we are not required to keep. Email support@bizloftgy.com and we will reply within 30 days.

Security

This site is served only over HTTPS. Passwords are sent straight to SphereMail and are never stored by us. Staff access to your records and your mail is limited to the people who need it to serve you.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell current clients by email before the change takes effect.
